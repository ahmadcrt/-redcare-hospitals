About REDCARE HOSPITALS

A fictional organization created for a software portfolio.

Overview

REDCARE HOSPITALS is an invented healthcare provider. It demonstrates a public website, appointment booking, a patient portal and an administrator dashboard backed by a secured database.

Fictional organization: not affiliated with any real hospital or company. Registration numbers, addresses and staff are made up.

Our approach

Prevention first, clear explanations, and respect for patient privacy. These are the values the demo is built around.

Mission

To show how accessible, trustworthy digital care services can be designed.

Vision

A healthcare experience where booking and records are simple and secure.

Core values

Compassion

Integrity

Excellence

Privacy

Facilities overview

Outpatient clinics, a diagnostic suite, a maternity wing and a day-surgery unit (all fictional).
